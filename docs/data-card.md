## Files and schema (observed)
Columns in both files: `Crop Name`, `Area Name`, `Area of Land in acres`, `Amount of Production in Tons`, `Rainfall in MM`, `Year`.
No temperature, humidity or season columns exist.

Canonical mapping used by the adapter: district = `Area Name` (stripped); crop = `Crop Name`; season = constant `annual_aggregate` (a label, not an observed field); year = `Year`; area_ha = acres × 0.404686; production_t = `Amount of Production in Tons` (assumed metric tonnes); rainfall_mm = `Rainfall in MM`; yield_t_per_ha = production_t / area_ha; temp_c and humidity_pct = NaN (not invented).

## Geography and years
Bangladesh, area-level records (68 distinct district-name strings after title-casing; spelling variants are **not** harmonised). Years 1970–2014.

## Cleaning and exclusion rules
Exact-duplicate removal (0 found); aggregate-geography exclusion; zero-area handling as above; range filter applied only to values that are present (yield 0–50 t/ha, rainfall 0–5000, etc.). The two rows removed as suspicious outliers: Barisal Jute 1988 (73.96 t/ha) and Patuakhali Jute 1988 (655.80 t/ha). Largest retained yield is 42.48 t/ha, which is high and was not investigated further.

## Unit assumptions (unverified)
Acres → hectares (×0.404686); "Tons" treated as metric tonnes; production/area treated as yield in t/ha.

## Intended use
Exploratory, retrospective yield-estimation research and pipeline demonstration, with human review. 

## Limitations / prohibited uses
Not field-validated; not suitable for operational advice to farmers, policy decisions, forecasting, or crop recommendation; no causal inference; no claim of official status. Coverage is limited to two crops and the listed years; no climate variables beyond the source's single rainfall field.
